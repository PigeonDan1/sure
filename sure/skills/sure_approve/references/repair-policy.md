# Repair Policy

Safe repair operates only on the isolated candidate snapshot. It may normalize portable derived paths and publication permissions, omit caches, virtual environments, old evaluation runs, and logs, materialize an explicitly declared resolvable asset link, or regenerate deterministic derived indexes and hashes from complete passing evidence.

Never change wrappers, servers, configs, model specifications, Dockerfiles, inference or adapter behavior, weights, payload contents, dependency locks, runtime contents, registry identity, failed validation, provenance, or license evidence. A finding in those categories requires a producer rerun.

After any repair, seal the complete candidate tree and rerun runtime verification. Verify that the producer source digest is unchanged before preparing review and again before publication.
